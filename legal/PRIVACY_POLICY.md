# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Graveyard Jokes Studios ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Lunar Blood (lunarblood.graveyardjokes.com).

## 1. Information We Collect

When you visit this site, we automatically collect:

- IP address and approximate geographic location
- Browser type and version
- Visit timestamps and page views

When you register an account, we collect:

- Email address
- Username and password (hashed)

When you use the contact form, we collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Message content

When you make a purchase or payment, we may collect:

- Payment information (processed by our payment provider)
- Purchase details and transaction history

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and maintain the service
- Manage user accounts (show and venue management)
- Process contact inquiries
- Process payments and maintain purchase records
- Understand site usage and improve performance
- Ensure security and prevent abuse

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Payment Processor** — for handling payment transactions (we do not store full payment card details)
- **Zoho Mail** — for email responses to contact inquiries

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Cookies

We use essential session cookies for authentication and CSRF protection. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details.

## 5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us. Payment records may be retained as required by law.

## 6. Your Rights

To access, correct, or request deletion of your data, contact us at [admin@graveyardjokes.com](mailto:admin@graveyardjokes.com).

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
